Volleyball Competition October 2022

ON THIS PAGE

In its 25th Anniversary Celebrations, TIDE congratulates the volleyball teams who endured under the sun and rain on October 16, 2022 to compete in the annual Julio Requena Volley Ball tournament. These young adults enhanced the celebrations by taking out time to submit videos of an aspect of TIDE’s work as their means of registration. We thank all of them! Special thanks to Mr Ray Palma for his continuous support each year in organizing the tournament and Mr. Daril Avila for his tremendous support. Congrats to our winners for the Volley Ball tournament.

Male Teams
1st Place won by Redz Team, Congrats to Anfernee Archer & Ian Selgado.
2nd Place won by Team Knee, Congrats to Camron Petillo & Raluni Bernadez.
3rd Place won by Team Rice and Beans, Congrats to Tybren Vialdores & Ricardo Requena.
Male MVP goes out to Ian Selgado

Female Teams
1st Place won by Team Defenders, Congrats to Key Ramclam and Janet Bejeranos
2nd Place won by Team Stars Times2, Congrats to Jasmine Faber & Miriam Adebara
3rd Place won by Team Jaguars, Congrats to Shania Garbutt and Jayleen Velasquez
Female MVP goes out to Keyla Ramclam
