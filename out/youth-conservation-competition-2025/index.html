Youth Conservation Competition 2025

ON THIS PAGE

 TIDE’s Youth Conservation Competition (YCC) 2025 will be held at 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM, October 11, 2025 in the Toledo Sports Auditorium under the theme “Restoring Healthy Ecosystems Through Action”.

This exciting event highlights the remarkable conservation efforts happening across Belize and gives a platform to our talented and knowledgeable youth. Contestants will demonstrate how their work contributes to restoring healthy ecosystems through action. (See highlights and photos from 2024)

We are inviting:

  • Candidates who are currently enrolled or planning to enroll in a tertiary institution in Belize.
  • Sponsoring schools or NGOs that will support candidates as they compete for scholarship prizes.

Candidate Requirements

  • Must be sponsored by a school or NGO.
  • Can be male or female.
  • Must represent their sponsor’s conservation efforts in alignment with the theme.
  • Should wear attire that reflects their sponsoring organization or school.
  • Must design and wear a creative costume made of at least 70% recycled materials.
  • Expected to participate in the sponsor’s community activities and outreach throughout the year.

Sponsor Responsibilities

  • Select a candidate from a stakeholder community who meets the eligibility criteria.
  • Submit candidate details to TIDE as soon as possible, including:
    • Candidate photos
    • Your organization/school logo
  • Cover all expenses for your candidate.
  • Help prepare your candidate to represent your organization effectively.
  • Support the costume design (concept and/or physical assistance).
  • Ensure the candidate is well-informed, well-prepared, and punctual.

Prize Money

$1,500 – First Prize

$1,000 – Second Prize

$550 – Third Prize

$300 – Consolation prizes for all other contestants
