Make a Lasting Impact: Help Protect Southern Belize’s Unique Biodiversity

Your support plays a critical role in preserving the stunning Toledo Landscape and Seascape, a vital ecosystem that supports endangered species and local communities alike. Every dollar you donate creates measurable, lasting change—whether it’s aiding in the conservation of the endangered yellow-headed parrot, safeguarding the delicate marine habitats of the endangered manatee, or fostering environmental education and sustainable livelihoods for the people of southern Belize.

At TIDE, we believe in the power of collaboration. Together we can build partnerships—whether with individuals, corporations, or academic institutions—that drive real results for conservation. By working hand in hand, we can do even more to protect the biodiversity that makes southern Belize a global treasure.

We invite you to join us in this mission. Your generosity will not only help conserve precious wildlife but also ensure that local communities thrive by sustainably managing their natural resources.

If you’re ready to make a difference, please reach out to our Acting Executive Director, Leonardo Chavarria, at execdirector@tidebelize.org or call TIDE at +501-722-2274. Be part of the rising TIDE that secures a brighter future for Belize’s natural heritage and the people who depend on it.

Your gift today is an investment in a future where biodiversity flourishes, and communities prosper. Let’s make it happen together.

To donate by credit/debit card: Please click on the Donate Button and select the Toledo Institute for Development and Environment as your designated fund. The page is on the website of our US fiscal depository, Philanthropic Ventures Foundation (501(c)3 Tax ID# 94-3136771). If you wish your donation to go to a particular TIDE program – reforestation, high school scholarships, yellow-headed parrot conservation or any other program – please specify this in the box titled “Add a further designation for your gift”. Our favourite kind of funding is flexible. If you’d like to leave it to us to decide how best to spend your donation, please leave the box blank. 

Donate To donate by check: You can send a check directly to TIDE. Please make it out to “The Toledo Institute for Development and Environment” and send to: TIDE PO Box 150, Punta Gorda Town, Belize, Central America 

If you are a US citizen and you wish for your donation to be tax deductable, please make your check payable to Philanthropic Ventures Foundation, our fiscal depository (501(c)3 Tax ID# 94-3136771). Please specify “Friends of TIDE Belize” in the check memo line and mail to: Friends of TIDE Belize c/o Philanthropic Ventures Foundation 1222 Preservation Park Way Oakland CA 94612 If you wish your donation to go to a particular TIDE program – reforestation, high school scholarships, yellow-headed parrot conservation or any other program – Specify this in a cover letter.
